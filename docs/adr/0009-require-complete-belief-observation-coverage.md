# Require complete belief-observation coverage for canonical development data

During the current reconstruction, a Canonical Game Bundle is eligible for Development Publication only when every alive observer has one successful Belief Observation at every PRE Boundary. Any exhausted speaker or non-speaker failure makes the game ineligible, while Canonical Failure Evidence preserves seed and game identity, boundary, observer, day and Public Phase, failure stage and category, and retry-exhaustion evidence so complete-case selection can be audited. Failed or missing observations retain `label_observed=false` and zero-placeholder meaning in raw failure schemas, but they do not occur in normal published development records; independent resampling, uniform fallback, silent row dropping, missing-rate thresholds, pilot mode, and generation of fallback trajectories are forbidden. A row-missing protocol would require a separate future scientific decision supported by evidence of systematic collection failure.
