# Create V1 speech actions only during Canonical Collection

V1 Speech Perception is the sole authoritative semantic transformation from raw public speech to `speech_annotations[].actions`, and it runs during Canonical Collection with bounded attempts. Each frozen annotation is identity- and digest-bound to its public speech and records its parser, prompt, model, backend, raw-response, attempt, version, and final-status provenance. An explicit successful `NONE` result is `status=no_action` with an empty action list and remains canonically eligible. Exhausted backend or parser failure is `status=error`, preserves complete failure evidence, and makes the game ineligible for Development Publication. Development Publication may verify one-to-one coverage, order, digests, versions, and successful status; Dataset may consume the frozen V1 actions. Neither may reparse, repair, replace, or fall back, and no shadow or V2 annotation path may supply canonical semantics.
