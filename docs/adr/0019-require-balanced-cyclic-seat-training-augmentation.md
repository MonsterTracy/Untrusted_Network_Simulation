# Require balanced cyclic-seat training augmentation

Primary fold training requires Balanced Cyclic Seat Augmentation. Across the immutable formal training budget, every training game receives balanced exposure to all seven cyclic player-ID shifts. The deterministic, versioned schedule is based on stable game and training-cycle identity rather than a temporary Dataset index or a generic `shuffle` flag, and paired Implicit and Explicit lineages use the exact same rotations. Each shift consistently permutes all public player references, V1 Speech Action subjects and objects, speakers, observers, belief-target rows and columns, Observer Alive State, and the already-derived Observer Eligibility Mask. It does not read Role Sidecar or recompute eligibility; temporal state, action and event semantics, chronological order, game identity, and fold identity remain unchanged. Formal Primary and All-Alive evaluation uses Canonical Seat Evaluation at shift zero without test-time ensembling. The mapping of balanced seven-shift coverage onto epochs, optimizer steps, batches, and loader mechanics is deferred to the implementation specification and may vary without weakening the invariant.
