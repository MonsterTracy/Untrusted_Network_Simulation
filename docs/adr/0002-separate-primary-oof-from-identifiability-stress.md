# Separate primary OOF from all-alive identifiability stress

Primary Development OOF trains fixed-budget terminal fold checkpoints and evaluates them over the `non_wolf_alive` population. All-Alive Identifiability Stress is a separate population-expansion evaluation over `all_alive` that reuses the exact Primary-trained checkpoint for the matching fold and temporal condition; all-alive rows never enter training loss or any training-protocol decision. Neither operation exposes a runtime supervision-scope choice, and their report identity, output lineage, and provenance remain distinct while explicitly binding the same checkpoint digest, so stress-test results cannot be mistaken for primary model-quality evidence. An experiment that trains on all-alive rows would be a separate future scientific program, not this stress test.

For each temporal condition, the formal Identifiability Stress Penalty is paired by held-out game and checkpoint. It subtracts that game's Primary mean belief KL from its All-Alive mean belief KL, then applies equal-weight game-macro aggregation. Positive values mean population expansion increased error; zero and negative values are reported without clipping. Uncertainty resamples game IDs and their Primary/All-Alive score pairs together in a paired game-cluster bootstrap. A ratio, subtraction of independently bootstrapped intervals, or role-specific third population cannot replace this definition.

Primary is not described as a strictly public-identifiable population. Belief Observation preserves the playing observer's legally available realized cognition, so non-wolf targets may reflect Seer checks or Witch private state that the public-only model cannot observe. Primary therefore asks how well public-only input predicts realized beliefs of alive non-wolf observers after excluding Werewolves with deterministic teammate knowledge. All-Alive is an incremental information-mismatch and population-expansion stress from adding those Werewolf rows, not a transition from entirely public-identifiable targets to the first presence of private information. No private-knowledge eligibility population or counterfactual public-only cognition is introduced.
